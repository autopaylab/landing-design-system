`TrustedByLogos` gets an optional `layout` prop: `"stacked"` (default, unchanged output) or `"split"`, which from `md` up puts the heading and description left-aligned in a left column and the logos at the end of a right column, vertically centred against the text. Below `md`, `"split"` renders like `"stacked"`. Per-logo `height` and `width` (max width) apply as before. New, not extracted: a page with only a couple of partner logos (paytalkpl) looked tall and empty with the stacked strip on desktop.

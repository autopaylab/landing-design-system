Optional props for single-goal landing pages: `Navbar` (`showAuthButtons`, `cta`, `languageLink`), `Footer` (optional banner, social links and scheme badges, button nav items, secondary logo), `HeroVideoSplit` (optional `videoSrc`, `mediaCaption`), `FourStepsSection` (optional `image`, `note`, three-column layout for three steps) and `CookieConsentScript` (`locale`). All defaults keep the previous output.
